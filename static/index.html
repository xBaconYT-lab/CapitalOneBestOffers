How the ranking works

  • Value = cashback ÷ the cheapest purchase that unlocks it. $20 back on an $8 plan is 2.5× back. $100 back on a $200 subscription is only 0.5×, so it is listed but not on top.
  • Min spend is an estimate. Capital One does not publish it, so it comes from the offer's own fine print when it states a threshold, otherwise from an editable table of entry-level prices. Click any amount to set your own; it is remembered in this browser.
  • Terms are read too. If the fine print says you must keep a service for 2 months, the min spend is doubled; "orders over $100" becomes the floor. Requirements like "new customers only" show as tags on each card.
  • Profit means the cashback is bigger than the purchase itself.
  • Your card offers. Capital One Offers (card-linked, capitaloneoffers.com) are personal and behind your login, so use "My card offers" to pull them from your own browser; they are ranked with the same rule and never leave your device.
  • Data comes from Capital One Shopping's public "Trending Offers" feed and refreshes automatically a few times a day. Offers are usually for new customers only; read the fine print before buying.
Not affiliated with Capital One. Estimates can be wrong; the merchant's checkout price is what counts.
